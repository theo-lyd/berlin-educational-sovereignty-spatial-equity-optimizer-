# Phase 12 Stakeholder Narrative

## Purpose

This narrative explains, in plain language, why the dashboard matters for Berlin public-sector education planning.

## 1. Why the District Gap Matters

The district gap is the difference between how many student places are needed and how many are planned.

If the gap is high, families and schools experience pressure first. Classes become crowded, travel distances may increase, and service quality declines.

For decision-makers, the district gap is a prioritization signal:
- where to allocate investment first
- where temporary solutions may be unavoidable
- where delivery delays create immediate social impact

## 2. Why Delay Matters

A project can look sufficient on paper but still fail to solve pressure if delivery is late.

Delay matters because demand is time-sensitive. A school place needed this year is not helped by capacity delivered years later.

The timeline and delay-risk views answer:
- which districts will remain under pressure in the short term
- which projects are likely to miss useful delivery windows
- where contingency planning is needed now, not later

## 3. Why Inclusion Matters

Special-needs provision is not optional. It is a legal and ethical obligation.

If special-needs demand is higher than planned special-needs capacity, districts face both service risk and compliance risk.

The inclusion page helps reviewers see:
- where inclusion coverage is below required levels
- where permanent provision is missing
- where intervention should be prioritized for equitable access

## 4. Why Temporary Site Costs Matter

Temporary sites can relieve pressure quickly, but prolonged dependence can become expensive and operationally inefficient.

When high-cost projects are delayed, temporary arrangements may persist longer than intended. This creates budget drag and reduces fiscal flexibility for permanent solutions.

The financial and interim-risk view helps stakeholders identify:
- districts with high temporary-site dependency
- expensive delayed projects with compounding risk
- where faster permanent delivery improves both service and financial efficiency

## 5. Decision Message for Public-Sector Reviewers

This system is not only a reporting dashboard. It is a decision framework that connects:
- demand pressure
- delivery timing
- inclusion obligations
- cost exposure
- data confidence

In practical terms, it supports better sequencing of investment and clearer justification for district-level planning actions.
